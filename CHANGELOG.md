### Added

- Frozen WitnessCell v14 condition-mean predictor.
- Dense/GO, sparse-self, and response-fingerprint endpoint evidence gates.
- Saturating factorized backbone and endpoint-incidence residual kernel.
- Exact and estimated Witness Risk APIs.
- Accept/abstain selective policy, including the explicitly retrospective
  Gate 21 Norman threshold.
- Typed API, CLI, AnnData adapter, safe `.wcell` model bundles, tests, release
  automation, anonymous-release audit, and reference-parity tooling.
- Unified constructor and fit-boundary validation for moments and leakage-safe
  splits, including strict finite-value and integer-count contracts.
- Strict Witness Risk and selective-policy configuration/input validation.
- Semantic `.wcell` state validation plus bounded outer and nested archives.
- Frozen golden predictions, a 67-test adversarial regression suite,
  branch-aware coverage, reproducible builds and SHA-pinned release actions.
