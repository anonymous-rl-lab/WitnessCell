- `core/experiments/shen/source/ORIGINAL_PIPELINE_LICENSE.txt`: source-specific notice, retained verbatim.
- Upstream packages installed from `requirements*.txt` retain their own licences; dependencies are not relicensed here.

- Research data redistribution and scientific-module permissions are distinct from the five-file interface licence. This archive does not infer blanket consent from a publicly downloadable paper or dataset.

The integration does not create a DOI, select a new project-wide licence, or publish a repository.
