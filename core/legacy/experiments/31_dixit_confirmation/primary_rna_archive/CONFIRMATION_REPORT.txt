SCOPE
This is a retrospective public-source evaluation with fresh training-fold parameters. No supplied development asset exposed this source; earlier author exposure outside those assets cannot be audited.
All predictor inputs, folds, gene axis, program, candidates and selection rules were frozen before model scoring.
A new-source partner gain, a biological hit-rate benefit, and a prospective validation have not been established.
Qualified external neural predictor comparison and biological uncertainty remain incomplete; the complete resource gate is not passed.
These findings do not negate earlier within-source results, but they bound the generality of those results.
