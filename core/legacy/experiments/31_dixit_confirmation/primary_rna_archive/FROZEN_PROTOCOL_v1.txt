WitnessCell v20 — independent-source RNA evaluation
Protocol v1, fixed before expression preprocessing and model evaluation

Purpose and scope
Test the frozen endpoint-plus-partner learning procedure on Dixit et al. (2016),
GSE90063 / GSM2396860, an experimental source not found in the supplied WitnessCell
development assets. This is a public-data, independent-source evaluation with
new training fits. It is neither a prospective experiment nor transfer of fitted
Wessels parameters. Source metadata and perturbation coverage have been inspected;
the UMI archive has been downloaded and its header read. No model performance on
this source has been inspected at protocol locking. Author exposure not recorded
in the supplied assets is not inferred from a negative filename/content search.

Source and labels
Use only GSM2396860 high-MOI K562 TF source CellRanger output and strict guide
assignments. Perturbed genes: CREB1, E2F4, EGR1, ELF1, ELK1, ETS1, GABPA, IRF1,
NR2C2, YY1. Retain exact two-guide cells with two distinct TF targets, with no
intergenic or third guide; 45 distinct pairs and 6,591 labelled cells before
expression QC. Singles have exactly one guide to one TF; controls are intergenic
safe-targeting guides, separately for exact one-guide and exact two-guide cells.
The metadata counts are 28,365 single cells, 8,802 one-guide control cells and
310 two-guide control cells. They are not biological replicate counts.

Preprocessing and no response-based eligibility
Retain source-provided cell barcodes with the above labels. Exclude only cells
whose total UMI over the full 23,529-gene source axis is zero. Apply no additional
HVG, differential-expression, phenotype, mitochondrial-fraction or observed-effect
filter. Compute per-cell log1p(10000 * UMI / total UMI) on the full axis, then
condition means and within-condition sample variances. Preserve unique ENSG IDs
and original symbols as separate columns; do not merge duplicate symbols.
The primary full-response axis contains every gene ID except the ten unique
perturbed TF coordinates. All predictors use this same 23,519-coordinate axis.
The kernel can use this same non-target response axis; it does not use test values.

For each condition, split cells deterministically within each source capture
suffix using SHA256 of seed 20261019, condition, capture and cell barcode, with
near-balanced assignment. Keep the assignments. Require each pair to retain at
least 20 cells in each half and all ten single endpoints to remain available.
Failure is a qualification stop, not permission to substitute other pairs.
Captures and cell halves are technical sampling units, not independent biological
replicates. No phenotype or response filter is applied after inspecting results.

Match guide multiplicity in the control reference. Let c1 and c2 be one-guide
and two-guide intergenic control means. The permitted endpoint effect is
e_g = mean(single_g) - c1. Supply the original mathematical core with control c2,
single means c2 + e_g, and measured double means. Rebase the cell-half inputs by
the corresponding half controls. Preserve the raw and rebased summaries so that
the adapter can be checked. This adapter is shared by every comparator.

Outer prediction task
Use the existing labels-only threefold candidate CSV. It is generated by the
round-robin one-factorization of the complete graph on the ten sorted TF names:
three consecutive perfect matchings form each fold. Each fold contains 15 test
pairs; each endpoint has degree three; incidence rank is ten, leaving five
endpoint-eliminated dimensions and three complete rectangles. The remaining
30 pairs and all permitted single/control cells form training. All cells, guides,
captures and both half measurements for a held-out pair are hidden from its fit.
Record the exact CSV hash before fitting. Do not change folds after expression QC
unless the entire evaluation is stopped and reclassified as development.

Core fitting when no original validation partition exists
Use the same complete mathematical v14 core on measured endpoints: elementwise
saturation v/(1+alpha*abs(v)), plus incidence-kernel endpoint compensation.
The absent-endpoint head has no effect because all ten endpoints are measured.
Choose alpha in [0,.02,.05,.1,.2,.5] and eta in [.01,.03,.1,.3,1] using threefold
pair-group cross-validation entirely within the allowed training pool. Estimate
the residual amplitude by the original clipped closed-form rule, using only
allowed training data. Select by full non-target MSE and refit on the allowed
training pool. Every nested invocation rebuilds the necessary core selection.
This training-only selection entry replaces the historical externally supplied
validation entry of Work A; it is explicitly part of the frozen adaptation.

Partner component and readout controls
Use the same projected, symmetric product kernel from the permitted single-
response profiles as in Work A, including consistent query residualization and
normalization. Train it on complete-core residuals generated by cross-fitting
within the allowed training pool. Choose ridge lambda [.1,1,10] using training-
internal predictions and the unit-weight non-target MSE. Then hold that fitted
G fixed for readout controls: zero correction, unit weight, uniform multiplier
from [0,.25,.5,.75,1], amplitude-only energy shrinkage with tau [0,.25,1,4,16],
and the unchanged half-measurement energy rule R. Uniform and amplitude tuning
use the same training-internal held-out predictions. Select the final policy
among those controls using only their training-internal full non-target MSE,
with a deterministic tie rule favoring zero, then simpler control. Do not enlarge
the grid after observing source performance. Independently optimizing a new G
for every control is not claimed by this shared-component experiment.

R is exactly clip[(||m||^2-||d||^2)/(||m||^2+||d||^2),0,1], m=(Z1+Z2)/2,
d=(Z1-Z2)/2, after training incidence projection. Count this formula and its
half-agreement expression as one method. Zero denominators yield zero weights.
Training-derived weights are shared over the evaluation queries.

Comparators and limits
Report measured-single additive, independently selected saturation-only, the
complete M0 core, every fixed-component control, and the training-selected final
procedure. The exact same outer information, gene mask and control adapter apply.
The pre-existing GEARS/scouter matched-55 comparison remains separate; those
predictions do not belong to this dataset. This execution does not declare a
new-source state-of-the-art result without a qualified external-model run.

Primary and secondary outcomes
Primary: pooled endpoint-eliminated squared-error gain of the training-selected
complete system over complete M0, 1 - sum_f ||P_f(Yhat_final-Y)||^2 /
sum_f ||P_f(Yhat_M0-Y)||^2, on the common fixed non-target gene axis.
P_f=I-X_f pinv(X_f), determined only by the held-out pair graph.
Mandatory secondary: pooled full non-target MSE gain, each outer fold separately,
and every predeclared control. Full error, endpoint-space error and orthogonal
error must satisfy the ordinary squared-error decomposition on the same axes.
Rectangle error is descriptive and is not substituted for the primary endpoint.
Report train-condition-variance standardization, with its scale calculated from
the allowed single means, training double means and double-control mean only;
sample variance across these conditions, floored at the first percentile of
positive variances. Report readout contribution concentration without using it
to redefine the principal gene population.

Predefined program secondary
Use only the 54 genes in PREDEFINED_G2M_PROGRAM.json, taken from Seurat's
cc.genes.updated.2019 g2m.genes resource, source SHA256
ffd7a3644f3137098d0d4b35c5ff654d70377a3ceab597b8159b0b9d16d84edf.
All directions are positive. Keep exact unique symbol mappings on the source
axis, remove any perturbed TF coordinate, and exclude ambiguous/missing symbols
without replacements. Require at least 70% of the original list; otherwise the
program endpoint is unavailable. The score is the mean of control-centered RNA
responses divided by each outer training-condition standard deviation using the
same 1% positive-variance floor. Report full program-response MSE and projected
program-response MSE. No phenotype mapping is learned, no outcome-driven program
is selected, and the score is not a measured hit rate or a direct mitosis assay.

Dependence, precision and decision interpretation
The panel has ten endpoints and fifteen total held-out contrast dimensions.
Neither 45 overlapping pairs, nine rectangles, 23,519 genes nor thousands of
cells constitute independent biological experiments. No 80% power assertion for
a 5% gain is justified from these counts. The finite source evaluation remains
informative about held-out prediction within this panel.
Report the primary point estimate, all three fold values, all ten leave-one-
endpoint evaluation sensitivities, and 10,000 fixed-prediction node-multiplier
reweightings with a fixed seed. For deletion or reweighting, rebuild the projection
on the retained/weighted query graph; never delete rows from the old projection.
Central 95% reweighting ranges are conditional sensitivity ranges, not calibrated
between-experiment confidence intervals or a substitute for biological repeats.

The approved resource criteria remain planning thresholds: at least 5% partner-
error improvement and no more than 2% full-response deterioration, together with
adequately supported uncertainty and a qualified strong-model comparison.
Point thresholds or conditional ranges alone do not mark that whole gate passed.
Report separately what prediction question is answered and what gate remains
unresolved. No biological-usefulness or prospective-validation claim is made.

Freeze and audit
Record actual UTC times and SHA256 for this protocol, preprocessing script,
gene-program list, outer fold CSV, runner and every locally imported scientific
dependency. Preprocessing may read the full public matrix; the model must receive
only its allowed rows. Freeze all outer predictions and selections before any
outer performance summary. Computational corrections are recorded; a change to
the statistical rule after scoring creates a new development analysis rather
than overwriting this evaluation. Keep inputs, logs, predictions and all outcomes.
