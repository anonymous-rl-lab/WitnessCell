WITNESSCELL V20 — WORK B: INDEPENDENT RNA SOURCE QUALIFICATION
Prepared 2026-10-19. Scope: source qualification and frozen preprocessing only.
No predictor was fitted or evaluated by this source-preparation workstream.

DECISION

The locked primary source is Dixit et al. (2016), GEO GSE90063, sample
GSM2396860, K562 TFs at high MOI. It supplies all 45 unordered pairs of ten
target transcription factors, measured single perturbations, and both one-
guide and two-guide intergenic controls. All 45 pairs pass the frozen minimum
of 20 cells per disjoint half; the observed minimum is 30. Prepared compact
inputs are ready for the separately frozen model evaluation.

This is a source-independent, retrospective public-data confirmation of a
learning rule. It is not prospective validation, an independent biological
replication within this dataset, or transfer of previously fitted weights.
Root's audit of supplied grn_repo_v23 experimental code/data text and v19
sources found no references to Dixit, GSE90063, or GSM2396860. This establishes
no recorded development use in the available assets, not an assertion about
unrecorded author exposure. The primary source was selected from metadata,
coverage, accessibility, and label geometry before expression aggregation or
prediction scoring. It must not be replaced according to model results.

Dixit et al., Perturb-Seq: Dissecting Molecular Circuits with Scalable
Single-Cell RNA Profiling of Pooled Genetic Screens. Cell 167 (2016),
1853–1866.e17. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2016.11.038
Primary full text: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5181115/
Study: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE90063
Sample: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSM2396860
Files: https://ftp.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/samples/GSM2396nnn/GSM2396860/suppl/

The primary Methods describe Cas9-expressing K562 cells, pooled lentiviral
guide delivery, and 10x Chromium RNA profiling. This is a Cas9 perturbation
experiment, not a CRISPRi experiment. Methods and GEO sample metadata specify
13 days after transduction for the later TF time point. Figure 6 describes
the later time point as 14 days, and barcode suffixes retain the original
ph14d names. We use the deposited sample identifier and report 13 days per
Methods/GEO, preserving source labels verbatim.

Eleven suffixes occur in the chosen sample's cell barcodes. The Methods
describe loading multiple Chromium channels, but no retrieved primary
annotation maps those eleven suffixes to independently grown biological
replicates. They are therefore used only as capture strata. We do not assert
that they are eleven independent cultures, nor infer biological replication
from the suffixes. Disjoint cell halves are sampling splits of this sample.

Access: the GEO files are publicly downloadable without credentials. No
dataset-specific Creative Commons license was identified in the retrieved
GEO record; no CC license is imputed. Preserve the study attribution and
public source URLs when redistributing derived summaries. The working copy
of the publisher/PMC article is for source verification, not part of the
redistributed computational data package.

2. WHAT WAS COUNTED FROM THE SOURCE LABELS

The deposited expression axis has 23,529 rows and 51,898 cells. The strict
guide-to-cell dictionary assigns at least one guide to 50,453 of these cells;
none of its labeled cells fall outside the expression axis. The remaining
1,445 RNA cells have no strict assignment and are excluded.

The guide dictionary contains 30 guide identities: active guides for ten TFs
and four intergenic controls. Eligibility is defined by guide identities,
not by collapsing arbitrary multi-guide cells into a gene-pair label:

* A double perturbation has exactly two guides targeting two different TFs.
* A single perturbation has exactly one guide targeting one TF.
* The one-guide control has exactly one intergenic guide.
* The two-guide control has exactly two intergenic guides.
* Active-plus-intergenic cells, two guides to the same TF, three or more
  guides, and unassigned cells are excluded.

The only additional expression-based exclusion permitted by the freeze was
total library UMI equal to zero. No otherwise-eligible cell had zero UMI.
No expression threshold, mitochondrial percentage, HVG, DE test, or observed
treatment effect was used to choose cells, genes, combinations, or folds.

3. LABEL-ONLY FOLDS AND THE ACTUAL INFERENTIAL SCALE

The locked three-fold split is the existing sorted-TF round-robin
one-factorization of the complete ten-gene graph, grouped into three
consecutive sets of three perfect matchings. Each outer fold contains 15
held-out pairs and 30 training pairs; all guides, cells, captures, and halves
of a pair remain together. The exact CSV is
Dixit_labels_only_threefold_candidate.csv, whose historical filename is
retained despite its adoption as the locked split.

In each held-out fold every gene has degree three, the unsigned endpoint
incidence matrix has rank ten, and the endpoint-orthogonal evaluation space
has dimension five. There are three complete rectangles per fold. Thus the
three folds provide 15 endpoint-orthogonal dimensions in total, not thousands
of independent cell- or readout-level replicates. The panel has only ten
perturbed genes. Fold, node, and readout sensitivity analyses in the locked
protocol are necessary to describe this scope; a favorable result cannot by
itself establish broad universality or biological-repeat uncertainty.

4. FROZEN PREPROCESSING AND DATA INTERFACE

Freeze: FROZEN_PROTOCOL_v1.txt, 2026-10-19 10:10:16.522 UTC.
Freeze receipt: PROTOCOL_FREEZE_RECEIPT.json.

Each source UMI count is converted per cell to
  log1p(10000 * UMI / total_library_UMI),
then averaged arithmetically within condition and cell half. Full and half
means are float64; unbiased per-condition sample variances include implicit
zeros. This is a declared normalized expression scale, not raw-count means.
All 23,529 source gene coordinates are retained.

Within each condition and capture suffix, cells are sorted by SHA256 of
20261019|cell_half|condition|capture|barcode and divided into disjoint halves.
An odd leftover is assigned deterministically by the hash of the condition
and capture. Each capture contributes as evenly as possible to the halves.
This creates sampling halves, not biological replicates.

The raw compact file contains 57 conditions: two control multiplicities,
ten singles, and 45 pairs. Raw count-weighted half means reconstruct full
means with maximum absolute error 1.7763568394002505e-15.

The ready compact file contains 56 conditions: control (the two-guide
control), ten singles, and 45 pairs. Single full means are rebased to the
two-guide control reference:
  ready_single = raw_single - one_guide_control + two_guide_control.
The same formula is applied separately using the corresponding controls
within each half, exactly as specified in the frozen protocol. Full means
and double-perturbation half means are unaffected by the implementation
correction described below.

The two-guide control and exactly-two-active-guide queries therefore share
guide multiplicity in the prediction reference, while single effects use
the matched one-guide control. This controls that defined reference mismatch;
it is not a claim that every guide-delivery or sample-selection confound has
been eliminated.

Important implementation correction before any prediction fit or score:
the originally hashed script added the full-control difference to both
single halves, contrary to the already frozen prose. The discrepancy was
identified and corrected to half-specific controls at 10:15:14 UTC. The old
script and old manifest are retained. IMPLEMENTATION_CORRECTION.json records
old/new hashes and the unchanged protocol hash. Raw source aggregation was
not repeated or changed. Ready single half means need not count-weight-
reconstruct the ready full mean because half-count fractions differ between
conditions; the maximum difference, 0.0008605780223616133, is explicitly
recorded, not mislabeled as an exact reconstruction. The frozen G/R inputs
use full singles and double halves, both unchanged by this correction.

5. DOWNLOAD SIZE, COST, AND PACKAGING

Source MTX gzip: 435,376,422 bytes; SHA256
cd9abbbe4dd080803440467945a1c93ebceba9c7601d087ed1a7c63c0902635f.
Its matrix shape is 23,529 x 51,898 with 154,803,161 nonzero entries.
Observed download time was 11.08 seconds. The two-pass streaming summary
completed in 116.30 seconds in this environment, without a dense gene-by-cell
allocation. Timing is an observed local cost, not a runtime guarantee.

The raw and ready condition summaries are about 24–25 MB each. Reproducing
the frozen prediction evaluation requires these sufficient summaries, gene
axis, pair splits, protocol, code, and receipts. Reproducing preprocessing
from original cells additionally requires re-downloading the public matrix
and the three small axis/strict-label files using their recorded hashes.
The original 435 MB matrix, failed-download fragments, full-paper working
copies, temporary R parsers, and backup RDS/pickle caches should not enter the
compact total code package.

cells improves statistical power and cost efficiency in Perturb-seq.
Primary DOI: https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.07.10.737863

One already processed MOI=0.5 pilot object was inspected only for metadata.
The downloaded RDS is 87,837,271 bytes and identifies an associated H5 assay
file; no H5 was downloaded. Its 17,671 cells all carry Biological_replicate=1.
Four sample identifiers ending rep1–rep4 therefore cannot be promoted to
four independent biological replicates.

The released assignment.fishash field is promoter-identity level, not raw
guide-index level. Exactly two different active promoter identities yield
889 promoter pairs and 3,284 cells. Only 30 pairs have at least ten cells;
none have twenty or forty. There are 45 single-promoter conditions with
11,229 cells and 609 NTC-only cells. These are our label counts, not model
results. This inspected object cannot satisfy the frozen requirement of
two disjoint halves of at least twenty cells per pair. Promoters were not
collapsed to genes and other MOIs were not pooled to manufacture eligibility.
No new backup downloads were pursued after that bounded qualification.

The backup remains an availability record, not a qualified replacement
confirmation panel. A failed or underpowered Dixit evaluation must be
reported at its actual evidence level instead of triggering a search for a
dataset on which the model succeeds.

7. HANDOFF LIMIT

This workstream has checked source identity, actual double perturbations,
guide multiplicity, gene IDs, cell coverage, fold geometry, normalization,
sampling-half assignment, and exact raw mean reconstruction. It has not
computed prediction accuracy, interaction magnitude, program performance,
or any other test-outcome metric. Success or failure of the frozen scientific
test belongs to the separate model-evaluation workstream and must be reported
regardless of direction.
