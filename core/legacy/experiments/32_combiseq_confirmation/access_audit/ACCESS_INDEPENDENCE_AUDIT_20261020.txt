A. Adamson 2016, GSE90546, DOI 10.1016/j.cell.2016.11.048
Status: distinct publication/study from Dixit, despite overlapping author teams; previously mentioned in the user's ACD public-data proposal, without a demonstrated expression-analysis record in the documents read. Absence of a WitnessCell hit does not prove never accessed by the team.
Design: primary article states all single, double and triple combinations of ERN1/IRE1alpha, EIF2AK3/PERK and ATF6. The commonly reused larger screen is primarily single-target.
Eligibility: the targeted double arm has three distinct pair identities on three genes. Its unsigned pair incidence matrix is the 3-by-3 triangle, determinant -2 and rank 3. Consequently n_pairs-rank(X)=0 and the endpoint-orthogonal response is identically zero. Guide multiplicity, cells, treatments and replicates cannot be counted as new partner pair identities. The present partner-primary estimand cannot be confirmed there. Treat as structurally ineligible without reading expression. Triple outcomes require a different task and predictor.

B. DLD1 / GSE337988 (Yeung, Tan et al. 2026)
Status: v20 SI Note 8.1 explicitly records labels-only inspection at MOI 0.5; no model evaluation before exclusion. A distinct-source candidate with recorded metadata exposure, not an unseen dataset in the literal sense.
Prior metadata: 889 exact-two-promoter pair labels, 3,284 cells, no pair reaching 20 cells; existing rule required 20 cells in each half. Replicate field was one. The same arm cannot be rescued by relabeling captures as biological replicates.
Source identity: author repository distinguishes new pilot/sublib2 data GSE337988 from a Replogle GSE146194 reanalysis. This distinction is critical; the latter cannot be counted as an independent experimental source just because it ships in a new paper/Zenodo package.
Next permissible step: inspect raw guide/condition metadata for other GSE337988 arms under fixed count, exact-two-target and biological-unit criteria before means or program outcomes. Select among eligible arms by fixed metadata rule, not error or G2M response. Do not globally regress out G2M/cell cycle in preprocessing when G2M is the frozen response target.

C. Compressed Perturb-seq, Yao et al., DOI 10.1038/s41587-023-01964-9, GSE221321
Status: separate source. No explicit retained-outcome record was found in this scoped WitnessCell retrieval; prior literature familiarity is possible. Raw-response access remains unverified, not certified absent.
Metadata: human THP1 macrophage-like cells, 598-target perturbation design, CRISPRi guide-pooled and Cas9 cell-pooled arms plus corresponding conventional screens. Published guide-pooled arm has 24,192 cells and mean 2.50 guides/cell; counts are design context, not a candidate-pair eligibility result.
Eligibility hazard: cell-pooling combines multiple cells in a droplet and must never be mistaken for two genetic interventions in one cell. Only guide-pooled or valid conventional exact-two-target singlets could enter the current task. Many pairs may have insufficient repeated cells; exact pair counts and endpoint-orthogonal graph dimension must be checked before expression aggregation. Author-inferred FR-Perturb interaction coefficients are not the heldout measured target for this comparison.
Scope: a new human cellular context, but G2M activity need not match proliferating K562. Do not choose a different program after inspecting this source's test outcomes and still call it the same frozen confirmation.
