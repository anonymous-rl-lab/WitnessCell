- All 44 pairs have matching TF+GFP singles within this same combinatorial arm.

CONTROL RESCUE CHECK (bounded to five minutes):
The scPerturb authors' converter constructs AnnData using tdata.raw.X, tdata.obs and tdata.raw.var from the original GSE217066 h5ad, then cleans TF strings and adds annotations. It contains no combinatorial-cell filtering step that could explain missing zero-perturbation controls. The original paper enumerates exactly 44 doubles, three triples and ten singles, matching our 57 conditions. The GEO SOFT metadata lists the same day-7 combinatorial libraries, with no separate zero-perturbation control sample. Thus no matched control rescue was established. Original 5.4-GB h5ad was NOT downloaded; no unsupported baseline was invented.

Design facts supported by the primary preprint/GEO:

- Six nominal MOI conditions: 0.1, 0.2, 0.5, 1.0, 3.0, 5.0, with direct infection and spinfection conditions. Do not equate MOI strata with independent biological replicates.

- Its independent newly generated pilot must be distinguished from the same paper's Replogle 2022 reanalysis (Zenodo 21232999); the latter is excluded from this confirmation.

REQUIRED METADATA GATE BEFORE EXPRESSION ACCESS
1. Verify guide assignment fields and promoter-to-gene mapping. Collapse guides by true target gene only under a fixed rule; two guides against one gene are not a double-gene perturbation.
2. Count cells with exactly two unique non-targeting-excluded target genes; separately record total guide burden and exclude/stratify additional guides by a predeclared rule.
3. Verify measured singles and non-targeting controls in the SAME infection/MOI/capture strata, including adequate single endpoints for the fixed-reference domain.
4. Determine whether enough unique pairs, endpoints, retained graph contrast dimensions, and cells per pair survive a prespecified minimum. Publish failures, not only a selected successful subset.
5. Check readout annotations for the frozen 54-gene G2M list without reading response values. A failure to map enough fixed readouts must not trigger outcome-guided program substitution.
6. Fix outer holdout gene pairs across all associated cells, guide variants and capture strata; endpoints may be observed via allowed single perturbations.
7. Avoid mixing different guide burdens or infection strata so that guide-loading effects on cell cycle masquerade as partner biology. The preprint already discusses guide-burden/cell-cycle effects. This is known background information, not a newly discovered WitnessCell result.
8. Record exact input downloads, hashes, access times, exclusions and frozen analysis before new expression or prediction evaluation.
