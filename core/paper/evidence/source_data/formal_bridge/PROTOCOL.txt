Fixed hidden-three-gate bridge experiment, 2026-10-31
Status: independently reviewed and frozen for one fixed training run.

Scientific scope
The estimator knows a finite candidate class of three single gates and the
deterministic upstream intervention semantics. It does not know which gate
generated a case or any outcome coefficient. This is a controlled information-
value experiment with hidden identity within that class, not unrestricted
mechanism-family or GRN discovery, a neural-model benchmark,
real-data confirmation, or an unrestricted-model identifiability theorem.

Mechanisms and panel
X~Bernoulli(.5), all six mediator variables equal X unless knocked out. Source
KO propagates to every descendant. Gates hA=(A OR B)(A OR C), hB=(B OR A)(B OR C),
hC=(C OR A)(C OR B). Outcome has intercept.7 and mediator nuisance coefficients
[.25,-.15,.4,-.2,.3,-.1]. Four fixed worlds: direct-only betaX1,betaH0; hA/hB/hC
each betaX0,betaH1. Learner coefficients are continuously valued with unrestricted
signs. True worlds are data-generator inputs only, never fit inputs.
Common control plus7singles X,A,...,F. High adds AB,BC,DE,DF; low adds AD,BD,BE,CF.
Both have12conditions, identical added endpoint degrees and equal cell budgets.
Unseen queries AC,AF,CE,EF are excluded from every training object. Rectangle
signs [+,-,-,+]; population target is -p*beta for hA/hC and0 for hB.

Budget and randomness
N256 per condition, balanced pre-intervention source strata128/128; source KO
turns both observed source strata to0. Independent Gaussian unit noise sigma1 is
synthetic and is not described as real biological noise. Exactly1000 fresh seeds
10000..10999; condition-specific noise shared across worlds/layouts, independent
between different conditions. Four worlds and two layouts are paired within
seed. The independent replication unit is1000seeds, not8000cases or cells.
Budget chosen before score from analytic minimum wrong-line distance N/8 and
prespecified joint bounds .06990,.70023,.95 for N64,128,256. No noise/strength sweep.

Fitting and sufficient statistics
For each candidate, fit9columns [1,Z1,...,Z6,X,hk] using identical unregularized
minimum-norm OLS. Count-weighted24stratum means are exactly sufficient for these
fits; within-stratum SSE is saved so full-cell SSE is recoverable. Select smallest
SSE, ties within absolute1e-10 whitened-stratum SSE use A,B,C order. All3candidate
coefficients/SSE/predictions are retained. Low has identical rank8 designs; its
unidentified coefficients are not restricted to three point estimates.
Predictions use the shared declared upstream/source prior; no query states,
query labels, hidden gate labels or true coefficients enter estimation.

Confidence sets, including refusal
Whiten design and means with sqrt(count)/sigma first. Project out the8nuisance
columns; the union of gate residuals has rank2 in high, rank0 in low. In the high
rank2 orthonormal coordinates, observed Z has unit covariance. Intersect the
chi-square2(.95) confidence ball centered at Z with each of the3gate lines.
Each nonempty line intersection yields an unrestricted signed beta interval.
Map via delta=-p*beta for A/C and via delta=0 for B. Report all candidate intervals
and exact mapped union; never condition the set on selected gate. Exact union
coverage is primary; hull only describes width and may fill gaps containing0.
Empty sets retain flags and NaN width, not0 width; they are incompatibility/
refusal, not nonzero certificates. A nonzero certificate requires a nonempty
union excluding0. Also separately report0-not-in-union including empty cases.
Low beta intervals are unbounded for all candidates: A/C delta are all R, B delta
is{0}. Store infinities in NPZ, not nonstandard JSON; never report finite low CI.

Comparators and scoring
Primary point-risk comparison: same3candidate OLS in high versus low, averaged
equally over4worlds within each noise seed. Every case contributes to point error
regardless of selection/CI/certification. Report world-specific results as well.
Strong high baseline uses observed-X1 R_AB,R_BC contrasts times p, covariance
[[2,1],[1,2]]/N. Fit the same3response lines with GLS and use the same joint ball
construction. Baseline unavailable in low; it never borrows AB/BC observations.
It predicts only rectangle, not whole query means. A delta0 auxiliary baseline
also predicts no whole-query means. No SOTA claim.
Metrics after freeze: population rectangle MSE, whole-query mean MSE for OLS only,
candidate selections, exact-union coverage, hull width and unbounded/empty rates,
and zero-target false certificates with empty separated. The direct-only and hB
worlds have zero rectangle. Confidence calibration is under the specified class.
Truth query means are independently enumerated only after prediction freeze.

Expanded-class boundary
Do not replace3single-gate fits with an11column simultaneous-three-gate regression.
In the high panel, hA+hC-hB-X is identically0, but its query rectangle equals-1
at p=.5. Thus that enlarged class is rank10 and the target is unidentifiable.
This analytic witness is preserved; the finite single-gate class restriction is
essential, rather than a result showing general causal identification.

Artifacts
One centralizedNPZ stores all8000cases: training means/counts/within-SSE, 3sets of
coefficients/SSE/query predictions, selections and all interval/empty flags.
cases.json identifies seed/world/layout and condition indices but is not supplied
to fit. condition_mapping.json and seed prefixes reproduce every Gaussian draw.
All files and model/configuration source are hashed before independent scoring.
